## Upstream and third-party attribution

This repository builds on the CapCap upstream project by its original contributors. Preserve upstream history and notices; see [upstream CapCap](https://github.com/notepower2k1/CapCap) and [LICENSE](LICENSE).

Libraries, models, binaries and voice assets are not automatically covered by the repository's Apache-2.0 license. Their source/revision/license/checksum must be listed in the release `THIRD_PARTY` manifest before distribution.

## Adding a contributor

After a contribution is merged, add the contributor's name, GitHub profile and contribution type (code, documentation, testing, design, review or maintenance) without exposing private contact information.
