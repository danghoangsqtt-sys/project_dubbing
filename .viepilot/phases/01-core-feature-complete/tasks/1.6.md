## File-Level Plan

- VieNeu adapter — expose pinned v3 Turbo provenance, serialize model inference, preserve native 48 kHz WAV output and validate synthesis.
- TTS processor — strengthen WAV validation to reject silence and apply it to VieNeu output.
- Timing helpers/workflow — clamp requested/per-cue speed to 0.92–1.12, default to a VieNeu preset, keep retry metrics/review state, and add a cue audition boundary.
- Tests/docs/state — deterministic WAV, silence, inference serialization, dubbing source, speed-bound and audition coverage with no model download.

## Best Practices

- One VieNeu inference owner at a time; release engine locks predictably.
- Preserve native sample rate until final mix; do not repeatedly resample.
- Cache/retry by normalized dubbing text and full voice/model provenance.
- Reject silence or invalid WAV as cue failure.
- Time-fit by rewrite/retry first and never exceed 0.92–1.12x automatically.
- Preserve unrelated user changes.
